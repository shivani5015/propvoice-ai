# Home Loan Guide

## Who is eligible for a home loan

A salaried buyer needs a minimum monthly income of 25,000 rupees. The CIBIL credit score should be 700 or above. Age must be between 21 and 60 years at the time of applying. Most banks ask for at least two years of continuous job experience. Self employed buyers need three years of income tax returns.

## How much loan will I get

Banks give a maximum of 80 percent of the property value as loan. This is called loan to value ratio. As a thumb rule the loan amount is about 60 times your monthly salary. So on a salary of 50,000 rupees per month you can expect a loan of about 30 lakh. On a salary of 80,000 rupees per month you can expect a loan of about 48 lakh.

## How much down payment do I need

You must pay at least 20 percent of the property price from your own pocket. The bank will never fund this part. For a 62 lakh flat the down payment is 12 lakh 40 thousand rupees. For a 45 lakh flat the down payment is 9 lakh rupees. Stamp duty and registration charges are also paid by you and are not included in the loan.

## What is the interest rate and tenure

The home loan interest rate is between 8.5 percent and 9.5 percent on floating rate. Women borrowers usually get a 0.05 percent lower rate. The maximum tenure is 30 years but most buyers choose 20 years. A longer tenure means a smaller EMI but much more total interest.

## What will my EMI be

On a loan of 50 lakh at 9 percent interest for 20 years the EMI is about 45,000 rupees per month. On a loan of 40 lakh at 9 percent for 20 years the EMI is about 36,000 rupees per month. On a loan of 25 lakh at 9 percent for 20 years the EMI is about 22,500 rupees per month. Banks want your total EMI to stay under 50 percent of your monthly income.

## Which documents are needed for the loan

You need PAN card, Aadhaar card, last three months salary slips, last six months bank statement, Form 16 of the last two years, and a passport size photo. For the property you need the sale agreement, the patta or title deed, and the approved building plan.

## Is there any government subsidy

Under the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana scheme a first time buyer from the EWS or LIG income group can get an interest subsidy of up to 2 lakh 67 thousand rupees. The buyer must not already own a pucca house anywhere in India. The property carpet area limit also applies.

## What are the extra loan charges

The bank charges a processing fee of about 0.5 percent of the loan amount. On a 50 lakh loan that is 25,000 rupees. There is also a legal and technical valuation charge of around 5,000 rupees. On a floating rate loan there is no pre payment or foreclosure penalty, so you can repay early for free.

Note: these are sample figures used for a demo project. Please check with your bank for the current rates.
